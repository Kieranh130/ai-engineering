The development project

A mobile shearing concept.

The proposed system is designed around a 44-tonne lorry and trailer. Sheep would enter at the rear using a ramp, move through a planned automated shearing process, and leave through a side exit nearer the front of the trailer.

01Rear loading
Sheep enter the trailer via a purpose-designed ramp.
02Automated process
The internal system is being designed to carry out the shearing process.
03Side exit
Sheep are planned to leave through a side door towards the front of the trailer.

Concept description only. The machine is currently in the design stage; no performance or safety claims are being made.
